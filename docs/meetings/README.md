## Standard agenda

1. Attendance, apologies and confirmation of quorum.
2. Review of actions from the previous meeting, each marked complete, in progress or overdue.
3. Progress against the sprint goal and the current milestone, using the board and the RAG status.
4. Blockers, risks and any change requests raised since the last meeting.
5. Work allocation for the coming period.
6. Any other business and confirmation of the next meeting.

Files are named `YYYY-MM-DD-meeting-type.md`. A blank template is in [_template.md](_template.md).

## Schedule pressures and how the team responded

All assessed submissions were made on Blackboard within their deadlines. The pressure the team faced was on its own internal task dates, mainly because other modules were running in parallel:

- **Stage 2 documents ran longer than planned.** The plan set an internal M1 review on 21 August, before the Blackboard dates were published. Costing, risk assessment and version control took longer than estimated, so when the submission links opened on 23 August the team aligned M1 to the 19 September deadline (CR-002) and finished the documents alongside Sprint 2. The pack and Gantt chart were submitted on time (MTG-03, MTG-05, MTG-14).
- **Design (M3, 11 Sep) - amber.** Because Sprint 2 was shared with the Stage 2 documents, the UML and DFD diagrams and remaining wireframes were not ready. M3 was passed conditionally on the approved database schema, and the diagrams followed on 23 September, 18 days after the internal target (MTG-08, MTG-10, MTG-16).
- **Development compressed into two long sessions.** To protect the M4 code freeze, the first integrated build was produced in two evening working sessions on 13-14 September. The branch and pull-request workflow was skipped to save time; corrective actions were agreed at the pre-submission review (MTG-11, MTG-17, CR-010).
- **Scope reduced at Sprint 4 planning.** US21, US33 and US35 were deferred and image upload (US25) was narrowed, using the MoSCoW contingency in the plan (MTG-15, CR-007, CR-008).

Despite these pressures, every Blackboard deadline was met, M4 was reached on time and all Must-have user stories were delivered.
